💳 How Our 3-Step E-Wallet Payment Works

We've made shopping simple, secure, and convenient. Here's how to complete your purchase using our E-Wallet payment system:

🛒 Step 1: Place Your Order

Add your favorite products to the cart and proceed to Checkout. Fill in your delivery details such as name, phone number, and address, then confirm your order.
No payment is made at this stage — you’re simply reserving your items.

💰 Step 2: Send the E-wallet

After placing your order, send your payment via E-wallet to 081 385 8181. Once received, we’ll get in touch to confirm that the payment was made by you.

✅ Step 3: Complete Payment with E-Wallet

Once you select E-Wallet, you’ll be directed to the E-Wallet Payment page. Log in to your wallet, confirm the payment amount, and authorize the transaction.
After successful payment, you'll receive a confirmation message, and your order will be marked as Paid.

💡 Tip: Make sure your E-Wallet has enough balance before confirming your payment.
