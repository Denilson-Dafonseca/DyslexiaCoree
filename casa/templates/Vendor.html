Vendor Partnership

Joining DyslexiaCore as a vendor

The life line of this site are the vendors of the public. All the products of the website belong to the people of the Walvis Bay community.

If you would like to partner with Dyslexiacore as a vendor, please follow the steps below. Vendors are individuals or businesses who supply their products to us for listing and sale on their behalf. To get started, send the details of all products you wish to list on our website to our WhatsApp number: 081 385 8181. Please include the following information for each product:

>Clear product images
>Your desired selling price
>A product description (if available)

There are no upfront fees to partner with Dyslexiacore as a vendor. Listing your products and gaining exposure to our large audience is completely free. We only charge a commission once a sale is made, at a rate of 10% per transaction. This is the only fee associated with selling on our platform.

Partnering with us is a mutually beneficial opportunity you gain increased visibility and revenue potential at no initial cost.
