We've made shopping simple, secure, and convenient. Here's how to complete your purchase using our Wallet payment system:

🛒 Step 1: Place Your Order

Add your favorite products to the cart and proceed to Checkout. Fill in your delivery details such as name, phone number, and address, then confirm your order.

💰 Step 2: Send the wallet

After placing your order, please complete your payment via cell banking using any bank of your choice and send it to 081 849 3832.

✅ Step 3: Payment verification

Once received, we’ll get in touch to confirm that the payment was made by you.

💡 Tip: Make sure your account's balance is sufficient when choosing to shop with us. .
