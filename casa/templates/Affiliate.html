Affiliate Commission

Make quick cash

Imagine earning money from the comfort of your own home simply by connecting people who are still selling their products on social media to a more efficient platform.

DyslexiaCore is introducing an affiliate commission program, designed with a unique twist. One of our primary goals is to make buying and selling goods within Walvis Bay more convenient and accessible. With this program, you can benefit by helping others transition from selling on platforms such as WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook to becoming vendors on DyslexiaCore.

How You Benefit If you refer someone to join DyslexiaCore as a vendor and they confirm that you recommended them, you will automatically become their affiliate partner. This code will be linked to the products associated with your referred vendor, allowing you to easily track your potential earnings.

This opportunity allows you to support local sellers while earning passive income just by making a recommendation.
